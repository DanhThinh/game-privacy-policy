## Information we collect

Our games do not require you to create an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or payment card details. The games and the third-party services they use may automatically collect the following information.

**Device identifiers:** Advertising ID (such as the Google Advertising ID) and other device or app instance identifiers.

**Approximate location:** country or region inferred from your IP address.

**App activity:** how you use the game, such as levels played, game events, and in-game interactions.

**Device and diagnostic information:** device model, operating system version, language, app version, crash logs, and performance data.

## How we use information

We use this information to provide, maintain, and improve our games; to show advertisements; to measure app performance, understand how players use the game, and fix crashes and bugs; to measure which marketing campaigns lead to installs; and to adjust game settings remotely, for example game balance and ad frequency.

## Third-party services

Our games use third-party services that may collect the information described above. These services have their own privacy policies.

## Your choices

**Advertising ID:** you can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in your device settings (on Android: Settings, Privacy, Ads).

**Consent:** where required by law, for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, we ask for your consent before showing personalized ads, and you can change your choice later in the game settings when available.

**Uninstalling:** you can stop all data collection by uninstalling the game.

## Children's privacy

Our games are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with information, contact us at the email below and we will take steps to delete it.

## Data retention and deletion

We keep data only as long as needed for the purposes described above. Because we do not operate player accounts, most data is held by our analytics and advertising providers under their own retention policies. To request deletion of data linked to your device, email us at the address below and include your Advertising ID if you want us to locate it.

## Data security
